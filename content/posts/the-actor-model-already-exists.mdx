## My first encounter(s) with Elixir

My first introduction to Elixir was in 2020 whilst was working on a "pro bono" project for a gaming community. The backend was in pure elixir, with heavy use of websockets and many different streams of data etc.

I, unfortunately, did not get to touch the backend much as I would have liked to, as this was during my frontend era (we all have to start somewhere); however, I did learn quite a bit about the actor paradigm as I had not experience anything outside of OOP.
