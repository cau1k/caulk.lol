I think "agentic" UIs will stop looking like chat within 12 months.

Instead, they'll look like:

- a logbook (decisions + diffs)
- a dashboard (queues, budgets, evaluations)
- a notebook (snippets, traces, postmortems)

This is mostly about accountability and iteration speed, not vibes.
